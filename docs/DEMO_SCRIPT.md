# 🎤 Pramanit Demo Day Script

**Time Limit**: 3 Minutes
**Goal**: Show complete flow from "Design" to "Verification".

---

## 0. The Hook (0:00 - 0:30)
*   **Speaker**: "Sending 1,000 certificates usually takes a week of manual work. Verification? Impossible. Today, we fix that with Pramanit."
*   **Screen**: Show the **Landing Page**. Scroll down to show the 3-step feature grid.
*   **Action**: Click **"Get Started"** and login (or use a pre-logged session).

## 1. The "Wow" Factor: AI Designer (0:30 - 1:15)
*   **Speaker**: "First, the design. We don't just upload PDFs; we build them."
*   **Screen**: Go to **Certificate Designer**.
*   **Action**:
    1.  Drag a **Text** element. Change font to "Cinzel".
    2.  Upload a **Logo**. Resize it.
    3.  **Key Moment**: Click the **"Pramanit Intelligence"** button (formerly AI Analyze). Show the popup: *"Design looks balanced, but logo contrast could be higher. Powered by Pramanit AI."*
    4.  Click **"Save Template"**.

## 2. The Power: One-Click Batching (1:15 - 2:00)
*   **Speaker**: "Now, let's issue this to 500 people."
*   **Screen**: Go to **Dashboard** -> **Issue Certificates**.
*   **Action**:
    1.  Select the template you just made.
    2.  Click **"Upload CSV"**. Select your demo file (`demo_recipients.csv`).
    3.  Show the **Queue Preview**.
    4.  **Vital**: Check the **"DPDPA Consent"** box. "We are legally compliant."
    5.  Click **"Launch Batch"**. Show the success toast.

## 3. The Trust: Instant Verification (2:00 - 2:45)
*   **Speaker**: "The recipient gets an email. They click the link..."
*   **Screen**: Open a **Verification Link** (have one ready in a new tab).
*   **Action**:
    1.  Show the **"Verified Credential"** badge.
    2.  Scroll to **"Share Hub"**.
    3.  Click **"Instagram Story"**. Show the image downloading.
    4.  "They can flex their achievement instantly."

## 4. Closing (2:45 - 3:00)
*   **Speaker**: "Pramanit is secure, scalable, and built for the future. Thank you."
*   **Screen**: Return to Landing Page or a "Thank You" slide.

---

### 💡 Pro-Tips for Demo
-   **Pre-load Data**: Don't type names manually. Have a CSV ready.
-   **Clean Desktop**: Hide your other tabs.
-   **Zoom In**: Standard web font is small on projectors. Zoom to 110% or 125%.
